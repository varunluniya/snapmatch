# Pre-Listing Review Policy

## Product type conflict
The photo shows a different product from the one described (for example, the listing says speaker and the photo shows headphones).
This is almost always a wrong-image upload. Hold the listing and ask the seller to re-upload the correct photo. Do not publish.

## Colour conflict
The listing names a colour family the photo does not show (for example, black in the listing and brown in the photo).
Colour is the most common reason for "item did not match description" returns in apparel and footwear.
Hold for review. The seller fixes either the photo or the colour in the title and attributes.
Shades within one family (crimson and red, navy and blue) are not conflicts.

## Material conflict
The listing claims a material the photo contradicts (suede and leather, stainless steel and plastic).
Material claims drive price expectations, so a conflict is a return risk and a trust risk. Hold for review.

## Low text similarity with no attribute conflict
The description and the photo caption share little vocabulary, but no attribute clearly conflicts.
This is often a thin or generic description. Publish only when the category's risk is low. Otherwise ask the seller to enrich the description.

## Category risk
Categories where a large share of returns are description mismatches (jackets, electronics) get a stricter hold threshold.
Categories where most returns are for size or change of mind (footwear, home goods) get a looser one.
Thresholds move automatically as return data arrives, in small bounded steps.

## Seller history
Sellers with repeated confirmed mismatches get a stricter threshold on every new listing until their record improves.
